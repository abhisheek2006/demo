Frequently asked questions

Is homoeopathy safe?

Safety depends on the product. Many highly diluted homeopathic products may contain little or none of the starting substance, but some products labelled homeopathic can contain active ingredients and may cause side effects or interact with medicines. Do not use homoeopathy to replace proven treatment or delay medical care. Ask a qualified health professional or pharmacist about any product you plan to use.

Can I take homoeopathic medicines alongside allopathic medication, or should I stop my current medicines?

Do not stop, reduce, or change any prescribed medicine without speaking with the clinician who prescribed it. Tell your doctor or pharmacist about any homoeopathic product you plan to use and show them its label; some products can contain active ingredients and may interact with other medicines. Do not let complementary products delay or replace proven care.

Can homoeopathy be given to infants and children?

Newborns and children should be assessed by a qualified paediatric clinician before any medicine is given. If a homeopathic product is being considered, discuss it and the child’s other medicines with the clinician. Do not delay or replace recommended paediatric care, vaccines or prescribed medicines. No product can be promised to be free of adverse effects.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring your previous prescriptions, investigation reports, and details of your past medical history and family history.

Is homoeopathy safe without side effects?

Homoeopathic medicines are highly diluted and, when prescribed in the correct potency and dose, are generally very well tolerated. "No side effects" is not the right claim, though — some patients notice a temporary change in symptoms after starting a remedy, and the remedy must be chosen for the right person. The real risk is delaying necessary medical care, which is why we always tell you when a condition needs hospital treatment.
